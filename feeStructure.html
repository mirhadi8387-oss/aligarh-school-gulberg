Fee Structure & Payment Guidelines

Academic Session 2025-2026 (Effective from April 01, 2026)

01.

Admission Fee & Initial Payment

Parents of successful candidates are required to collect the first fee challan from the Accounts Department of the school. The first fee challan form includes charges for a security deposit (refundable at the time of withdrawal only), admission fee, one month’s fee, and any other applicable charges[cite: 1]. When all the dues are deposited in the authorized banks, the admission procedure gets completed and the child may join the class.

The school fee is payable for all twelve calendar months. The school retains the discretion to review and increase school fees and all other charges with or without any prior written notice.

03.

Additional Charges & Funds

Admission Test Fee

Rs. 700/- (Non-Refundable)

Admission Fee

Equals to Monthly Tuition fee (Non-Refundable)

Security Deposit

Equals to full Monthly fee (Tuition + Funds). Refundable within 6 months from leaving, else credited to Tehzibul Akhlaq Trust as donation.

Examination & Annual Magazine

Exam Fee: Rs. 700/- per terminal exam. Annual Magazine: Rs. 700/- (As per publication).

04.

Important Guidelines & Payment Rules

  • Authorized Banks & Due Date: Last date for receipt of fee direct in Muslim Commercial Bank Ltd. (Liberty Market Branch, Gulberg) and UBL (Main Market) is the 10th day of every month.
  • Late Fine: Fee paid after the 10th will be received by the bank with a late fee fine of Rs. 25/- per day up to the 20th day of the month.
  • Default Action: If fee is not paid till the 20th day of the month, the student's name will be struck off the School / College rolls. If re-enrolling after being struck off due to non-payment, a Default Fee of Rs. 1000/- will be charged in addition to late fines.
  • Duplicate Challan: Rs. 150/- will be charged for every duplicate fee challan.
  • Mid-Term Joiners: Students joining mid-term without a school leaving certificate will be charged full fee and funds from April 1st.
  • Vacation & Seasonal Dues: Fee for February & March is payable in February. May & June fee is payable in May. July and August fee is payable in their respective months.
  • Withdrawal Notice: One month's notice in advance is required for student withdrawal, otherwise school authorities reserve the right to demand one month's tuition fee and funds in lieu thereof.
