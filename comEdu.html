Digital Empowerment

Computer Education (K.G. – X)

Preparing Students for Modern Practical Life through Tech Skills & Digital Literacy

Essential Tech Skills for Smart Work

Computer education has become essential for smart work in every walk of life. Realizing its vital importance in practical careers, we teach Computer Studies across all levels—from Kindergarten to Class X—equipping students with real-world technical competence.

01
Foundational Knowledge

Enabling students to learn basic computer concepts, creating a strong foundation to seamlessly move towards higher education with solid prior technical knowledge.

02
Hands-on Lab Practice

Providing ample opportunities for testing application skills through hands-on practice in separate, dedicated Junior & Senior Computer Labs.

03
Multimedia Presentations

Fostering creative interest in tech studies by assigning diverse presentation topics to build confidence and multimedia communication skills.

04
Guided Internet Research

Developing healthy research habits among senior students through teacher-guided internet surfing and real-time project tasks.

Faculty Excellence

Continuous Professional Development (CPD)

We believe that effective teachers make a profound difference in the lives of students. To ensure classroom effectiveness, we continuously provide opportunities for learning through short courses, seminars, and specialized training workshops.

Key Focus Areas of Teacher Training:
📖
Curriculum Understanding

Effective understanding and smooth implementation of national curriculum.

🎯
Teaching Strategies

Modern pedagogical methods and student-centric learning techniques.

🏫
Classroom Management

Creating structured, encouraging, and discipline-oriented environments.

🌟
Character Building

Focusing on personal development and moral grooming of educators.

We express our sincere gratitude to Tehzibul Akhlaq Trust (TAT) for bearing all financial expenses of these vital teacher training sessions.

ALIGARH PUBLIC SCHOOL

The mission of Sir Syed Ahmad Khan, Tehzibul Akhlaq Trust was established in 1961 at Lahore by the enlightened, progressive and devoted alumni of Aligarh Muslim University and some other eminent scholars and philanthropists.
