A Space for Digital Excellence

The Senior Boys Computer Lab supports students in developing their computer knowledge and practical understanding of modern technology.

Through structured lessons and hands-on activities, students are encouraged to explore computer applications, understand digital concepts and improve their problem-solving abilities.

Our aim is to provide a learning environment where technology becomes an effective tool for academic growth, creativity and future opportunities.
