Separate Campus

Aligarh Public High School for Boys (Sr. Wing)

Empowering Boys through Focused Mentorship & Individual Aptitude Development

Classes V – X Established 2008

Dedicated Senior Boys Wing

In 2008, Aligarh Public High School for Boys was established in an entirely separate campus at Gulberg, serving as a starring addition to the educational institutions operating under the auspices of Tehzibul Akhlaq Trust.

At the Boys School, students are provided with structured opportunities to grow and develop according to their own aptitudes and unique abilities.

✓
Personalized Guidance & Counseling

One-on-one counseling and personal guidance are provided to students whenever required to ensure academic success and moral growth.
