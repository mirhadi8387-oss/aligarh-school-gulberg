About Our Chemistry Laboratory

The Chemistry Laboratory at Aligarh Public School & College provides students with an opportunity to explore scientific concepts through practical experiments and observation. It supports classroom learning by helping students understand the properties, composition and reactions of different substances.

Through practical activities, students develop scientific curiosity, analytical thinking and problem-solving skills. The laboratory encourages them to observe, investigate and understand the principles of chemistry in a practical setting.

Our aim is to make science learning engaging, meaningful and connected to real-life experiences.

Laboratory Safety and Responsibility

A safe laboratory environment is essential for effective practical learning. Students are encouraged to follow laboratory instructions, handle equipment carefully and maintain cleanliness and discipline during practical activities.
