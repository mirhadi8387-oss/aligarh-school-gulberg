A WARM WELCOME

Welcome to Aligarh Public School Gulberg,

It is with great pleasure that I welcome you to this truly special school. As you read through the pages of this website, you will learn about our wonderful students, our School, and get a feel for the vibrancy and strength of our wider AGPS community.

Aligarh Public School Gulberg is one of the leading providers of Higher Secondary education to girls and Secondary education to boys at separate campuses. It offers students a vibrant atmosphere and a strong sense of community. In such a supportive environment, each student feels happy, cared for and valued as an individual.

We seek to bring out the best in each of our students who are encouraged to develop their potential in many ways. Work in the classroom stimulates their intellect and shows them what the world has to offer. The creative and sporting talents of students are nurtured and encouraged, whilst the extensive range of extra-curricular activities challenge and absorb individual potentials.

Our academic record is outstanding and students are taught not just how to achieve excellent examination results, but how to become a good human being with high moral standards. We believe firmly in educating the whole person and each child’s moral and social development is highly valued. This is reflected in the school ethos which is based on tolerance, respect and responsibility.

Students leave AGPS as well-educated, young men and women with highly-developed social skills and a broad range of interests. Our students are confident in their own worth, prepared for an independent life and capable of making a positive contribution to society. It is from this cornerstone that they go on to pursue varied and fulfilling careers.

This website will only give you a glimpse of the special opportunities we offer to our students. The best way to find out more about Aligarh Public School Gulberg is to visit us on any weekday by appointment. We look forward to welcoming you to the School.

With best wishes,

Mrs. Mudasra Hasan

Principal — Aligarh Public School Gulberg

Learning Today, Leading Tomorrow

Together, we strive to nurture responsible, confident and compassionate individuals prepared to contribute positively to society.
