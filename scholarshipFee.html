Financial Support & Relief

Scholarships & Fee Concessions

Committed to Supporting Deserving Families and Meritorious Students

01.

Grounds for Concessions in Tuition Fee

Concessions in tuition fees are granted under specific criteria to assist families and deserving students:

Family Support
Kinship Basis

Half tuition-fee concession is granted to every 2nd and 4th child with the same parentage.

Special Relief
Orphans

Full tuition-fee concession is granted upon the production of the death certificate of the real father.

Staff Benefit
Employees’ Children

Full tuition-fee concession is provided to the children of school / Trust employees.

Need-Based
Poverty / Financial Basis

Granted to most deserving students based on financial position, academic results, living standards, and utility bills. Varies from 10% to 100% under an approved 10% institutional quota.

02.

Fee Concession Rules & Policies

  • Duration & Renewal: All concessions are granted for one academic year only. They may be renewed for the next year provided the student maintains consistency in good results.
  • Application Timeline: Applications for fee concessions must be submitted at the beginning of the session or at the time of admission. Concession forms are available at the school office.
  • Withdrawal of Concession: Concessions shall be immediately withdrawn in case of student misconduct or failure to show good academic results.
  • Principal’s Authority: The Principal of the respective school or college determines the exact amount of tuition fee concession granted on poverty basis.
  • Tuition Only: Fee concessions are awarded only in tuition fees and not in funds. All mandatory funds must be paid in full by every student.
