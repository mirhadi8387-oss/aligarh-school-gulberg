About Our Physics Laboratory

The Physics Laboratory at Aligarh Public School & College provides students with an opportunity to explore the fundamental principles of physics through practical experiments, demonstrations and scientific observations.

It helps students connect theoretical concepts with real-world applications by exploring topics such as motion, force, energy, electricity, magnetism, light and sound.

Through hands-on activities, students develop analytical thinking, measurement skills, curiosity and a deeper understanding of the physical world around them.

RESPONSIBLE SCIENCE LEARNING

Laboratory Safety & Responsibility

Safety and discipline are important parts of practical science education. Students are encouraged to follow instructions, handle laboratory instruments carefully and perform experiments under appropriate supervision.

Follow laboratory instructions.
Handle instruments and equipment carefully.
Observe electrical safety precautions.
Maintain cleanliness and discipline.
