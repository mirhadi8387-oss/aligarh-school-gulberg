Institutional Regulations

Student Withdrawals Policy

Guidelines and Formal Procedures Regarding Student Withdrawal from School

01.

Voluntary Withdrawal & Notice Requirement

If parents wish to withdraw their child from the school, one month's notice in writing must be given to the school.

Alternatively, one month's fee must be paid to the school in lieu of such notice.

02.

Conditions for Automatic & Disciplinary Withdrawal

Moreover, a student shall be considered officially withdrawn from the school under any of the following circumstances:

a) Prolonged Absence

Absence from the school for a continuous period of one month without any prior approval by the head of the institute.

b) Non-payment of Dues

Non-payment of school dues for two consecutive months.

c) Breach of School Policy & Disciplinary Action

Breach of the school policy which may lead to disciplinary action against the child. In the event of such a withdrawal, the school shall not be required to give any notice before enforcing the decision, which shall remain within the absolute and sole discretion of the Principal.
