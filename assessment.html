Evaluation System

Assessment & Academic Year

Structured Evaluation, Paced Learning & Parent Performance Reporting

01
1st Term

Regular Instructions + Paced Coverage + 2 Weeks Revision followed by 1st Term Examinations.

02
2nd Term

Comprehensive Learning + 2 Weeks Revision followed by Mid-Year 2nd Term Examinations.

03
Annual Term

Full Syllabus Review + 3 Weeks Intensive Revision prior to Final Annual Examinations.

Instructions & Revision Strategy

The academic year is systematically divided into three terms. Within each term, dedicated times are allocated for regular instructions, revisions, and examinations.

📘 Regular Instruction Period

Students learn new concepts in each subject following strict pacing charts set by the management.

🔄 Paced Revision Phase

Revision time is comprehensive yet selective. 2 weeks of revision precede 1st and 2nd Term exams, while 3 full weeks of revision are conducted before Annual Exams.

Progress & Parent Reporting

During the academic year, parents receive detailed performance feedback to stay fully informed about their child's educational journey.

📊 Monthly & Term Reports

Reports are issued after monthly tests and at the end of each term. The first two term reports provide an in-depth record of term performance.

🎓 End-of-Year Final Report

The final end-of-year report highlights performance across the entire school year and contains essential criteria related to class promotion.
