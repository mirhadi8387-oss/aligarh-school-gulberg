About Our Biology Laboratory

The Biology Laboratory at Aligarh Public School & College provides students with an opportunity to explore the fascinating world of living organisms through practical activities, observation and scientific investigation.

It supports classroom learning by helping students understand the structure and functions of living things, cells, plant and animal tissues, human body systems and biological processes.

Through hands-on learning and microscopic observation, students develop scientific curiosity, analytical thinking and a deeper appreciation of life and nature.

OUR LABORATORY

Exploring Life Through Science

Practical activities encourage students to investigate biological structures, processes and the diversity of living organisms.

Microscopic Studies

Introducing students to microscopic observation and the study of cells and biological structures.

Plant Studies

Exploring plant structures, tissues, growth and the biological processes of plant life.

Cell & Tissue Studies

Understanding the organization of cells, tissues and their important functions in living organisms.

Human Biology

Learning about the human body, its organ systems and essential biological functions.

RESPONSIBLE SCIENCE LEARNING

Laboratory Safety & Responsibility

Maintaining a clean and safe laboratory environment is essential for effective biological learning. Students are encouraged to follow instructions, handle laboratory materials carefully and maintain proper hygiene during practical activities.

Follow laboratory instructions.
Handle specimens and equipment carefully.
Maintain cleanliness and personal hygiene.
Perform practical work under proper supervision.
