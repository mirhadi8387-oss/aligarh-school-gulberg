About Senior Boys Branch

The Senior Boys Branch of Aligarh Public School & College is dedicated to providing students with a supportive, disciplined and academically focused learning environment. We aim to prepare students for higher education and the challenges of an evolving world.

Our approach focuses on academic excellence, personal responsibility, confidence and character building. Through effective teaching, guidance and meaningful learning experiences, we encourage students to recognize their potential and strive for continuous improvement.
