OUR PURPOSE & VALUES

Our Mission Statement

Empowering students for academic, moral, and personal excellence.

OUR COMMITMENT

Our Mission Is to Inspire, Educate & Empower

We strive to nurture confident, responsible, and capable individuals through quality education and strong moral values.

01

Developing Potential

To develop the inherent potential and talent of the students by educating them in various Modern Disciplines.

02

Cultural Identity

To give students confidence in their own cultural identity.

03

Islamic Values & Character

To groom their personalities within the framework of Islamic values in order to achieve academic, social, and national excellence.

04

Healthy Living

To develop their physical skills and instill in them an appreciation of the need for healthy living through participation in sporting and recreational activities.

05

Inquiry, Tolerance & Morality

To foster in students the habits of “free inquiry, large-hearted tolerance, and pure morality”.

06

Quality Education

To provide high-quality teaching through the recruitment of well-qualified and experienced staff in line with the educational standards set by the Ministry of Education.

Building knowledge, nurturing character, and preparing students for a brighter future.

ALIGARH PUBLIC SCHOOL

The mission of Sir Syed Ahmad Khan, Tehzibul Akhlaq Trust was established in 1961 at Lahore by the enlightened, progressive and devoted alumni of Aligarh Muslim University and some other eminent scholars and philanthropists.
