A LEGACY OF LEARNING

The Vision Behind the Aligarh Movement

A journey of educational awakening, intellectual growth and progress.

THE FOUNDER'S VISION

Sir Syed Ahmed Khan

Sir Syed Ahmed Khan—a literary genius, an outstanding scholar, an educationist, jurist and politician— pioneered modern education for the Muslim community in India and founded the Aligarh Muslim University. His work gave rise to a new generation of Muslim intellectuals, politicians and the Aligarh Movement to secure the political future of Muslims in British India.

THE VISION

The Vision (Post-1857)

In the aftermath of the Indian Rebellion of 1857, Sir Syed foresaw the imperative need for Muslims to acquire proficiency in the English language and "Western Sciences" to maintain their social and political clout, backed by his influential booklet “Asbab-e-Baghawat-e-Hind”.

EDUCATIONAL AWAKENING

Awakening & Institutions

He started publishing the journal “Tehzibul Akhlaq” in 1870, founded MAO College in 1877, which was transformed into Aligarh Muslim University in 1920—widely believed to be “the University that created Pakistan”.

He began to prepare the roadmap for the formation of a Muslim University by starting various schools. Over the years, the MAO College gave rise to a new educated class of Muslims who were politically active in the British Raj.

Inspired by a Vision. Guided by Education.

Preserving the spirit of educational progress and intellectual excellence.

ALIGARH PUBLIC SCHOOL

The mission of Sir Syed Ahmad Khan, Tehzibul Akhlaq Trust was established in 1961 at Lahore by the enlightened, progressive and devoted alumni of Aligarh Muslim University and some other eminent scholars and philanthropists.
