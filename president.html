President's Message

A Message from the Leadership of Tehzibul Akhlaq Trust

A WORD FROM OUR PRESIDENT

Building on a Legacy of Educational Excellence

A vision of education, service and character development for generations to come.

OUR PRESIDENT

G.A. Sabri

President

Tehzibul Akhlaq Trust (TAT)

Assalam Alaikum,

As you browse through our website, I invite you to imagine a group of dedicated men, inspired by the ideals of their alma mater Aligarh Muslim University and fired with the desire to serve their nation, getting together to strive for the education of youth. They did not have the resources to translate their dream into reality, but their enthusiasm was infectious; they were able to gather enough support and followers over a span of fifty years to lead us to where we are today: a vibrant institution where over 20,000 have passed through its portals and over 2,000 are gaining knowledge and learning to be good human beings and patriotic citizens.

We are a group of volunteers who aim to bring good quality education at a reasonable cost. We also offer hostel facilities at our Manga Campus for those who live far. Our custom-built campuses offer big airy classrooms, labs, playgrounds, library, and much more. However, more than just physical facilities, we link our youth to a glorious historical past, we teach them values, and we strive to equip them to deal with life.

As you take a virtual tour of our institution, you will see that we are not a static group. We have been constantly growing and we have ambitions to grow further. Come and be a part of this growth process and you will have the satisfaction of sharing a noble cause.

With best wishes for our shared mission

Continuing the Mission of Education

Together, we can nurture knowledge, character and responsible citizens for a better future.

ALIGARH PUBLIC SCHOOL

The mission of Sir Syed Ahmad Khan, Tehzibul Akhlaq Trust was established in 1961 at Lahore by the enlightened, progressive and devoted alumni of Aligarh Muslim University and some other eminent scholars and philanthropists.
