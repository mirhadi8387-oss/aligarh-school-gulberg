OUR PURPOSE

Our Objectives

Guiding Goals for Educational and Institutional Excellence

01

Affordable Quality Education

To provide quality education at an affordable cost, making high standards accessible to all segments of society.

02

All-Round Student Development

To focus on the all-round development and welfare of students by assuring good health, social development, and strong character building.

03

Teacher Professional Growth

To ensure the professional growth of teachers by providing opportunities to refresh knowledge and acquire new experiences through workshops and in-house training sessions.

04

National Curriculum Alignment

To achieve all institutional objectives effectively within the framework of the national curriculum.

Building a Better Future Through Education

Promoting knowledge, character, personal growth and excellence.

ALIGARH PUBLIC SCHOOL

The mission of Sir Syed Ahmad Khan, Tehzibul Akhlaq Trust was established in 1961 at Lahore by the enlightened, progressive and devoted alumni of Aligarh Muslim University and some other eminent scholars and philanthropists.
