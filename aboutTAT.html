OUR HISTORY & PURPOSE

Tehzibul Akhlaq Trust

In furtherance of the mission of Sir Syed Ahmad Khan, Tehzibul Akhlaq Trust was established in 1961 at Lahore by the enlightened, progressive, and devoted alumni of Aligarh Muslim University, along with other eminent scholars and philanthropists.

OUR COMMITMENT

Key Objectives of the Trust

01
Preserving Islamic Education & Cultural Values

Ensure advancement and preservation of Islamic education and cultural values.

02
Establishing Educational Institutions

Set up educational institutions on the lines of the historic Muslim University, Aligarh.

Continuing a Vision Through Education

Preserving the legacy of Sir Syed Ahmad Khan through learning, values and educational opportunities.
