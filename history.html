OUR LEGACY

Milestones That Shaped Our Journey

From a rented building to an expanding educational institution, our journey reflects dedication, resilience and a commitment to quality education.

1965 – 1982

Started in a Rented Building

On 6th September 1965, about 75 children entered the premises of Aligarh Public School, which started in a rented building at 39-B/1, Gulberg-III, Lahore. Unfortunately, this day coincided with the outbreak of the 1965 war between India & Pakistan at the Wahga Border. Although the school remained closed for a few days, academic activities promptly resumed after the war.

Through the untiring efforts of management and staff, the school grew rapidly. In 1966, it shifted to a larger building at 27-C-I, Gulberg-III, serving as the sole institute in the vicinity catering to K.G., Primary, Middle, and Secondary levels.

Education is the knowledge not of facts but of values.

12th Sep 1982 – Present

Shifted to Custom-Built Campus

To fulfill pressing educational demands, the school shifted to its current permanent campus on 12th September 1982. Built on a 14 Kanals, 12 Marlas, and 90 sq. ft. land purchased from LDA in 1968, this purpose-built facility marked a turning point in the institute's trajectory.

Honoring Our Founders
“We would not have been what we are today without the missionary zeal and untiring efforts of our founding fathers.”

Maintaining steady quality education, the school upgraded to the Higher Secondary Level in 1991.

GROWTH & EXPANSION

Expanding Educational Opportunities

April 2006

Inauguration of College for Women

A new purpose-built campus for intermediate classes was inaugurated on 12th April 2006 by Mr. Abdur Razak Dawood (Ex-Federal Minister, Chairman DESCON & Rector LUMS). The Chief Guest commended TAT’s service in promoting female education.

2008

Aligarh High School for Boys (Sr. Wing)

In 2008, an entirely separate campus for boys was launched. Designed to nurture individual aptitudes, the campus provides structured academic programs alongside one-on-one counseling and personal guidance.

A Legacy of Learning and Progress

Our history reflects a continuing commitment to education, institutional growth and the development of future generations.
