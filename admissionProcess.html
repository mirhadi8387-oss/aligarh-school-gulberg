Admission Procedure & Guidelines

Your straightforward pathway to enrollment from Play Group to Class IX

01.

Registration Process

The admission procedure begins with registration, which in itself does not guarantee admission. Registration usually starts in the month of February each year. A registration fee is charged to cover clerical and administrative costs. Upon payment of this fee, the school office issues a receipt and a registration form to be filled in by the parents or guardian.

02.

Admission Tests & Diagnostic Assessment

Diagnostic admission tests are normally held in March of each year. All students from K.G. to IX seeking admission must take diagnostic tests in core subjects: English, Maths & Urdu. These tests determine if prospective students have attained the minimum expected standard.

Note: Final placement regarding class and section is made solely on performance. Parents and students do not have a choice of section; only the school Management allots sections as per institutional policy.

03.

Early Years Assessment (Play Group & Nursery)

As such, there is no diagnostic test requirement for admission to Play Group and Nursery. Instead, specific developmental criteria are evaluated:

Play Group Level

Prospective students participate in a brief interaction. They must be able to speak, follow simple instructions, and be completely toilet trained.

Nursery Level

Prospective students must demonstrate the ability to recognize English and Urdu alphabets, numbers, primary colors, and maintain a proper grip on a pencil.
