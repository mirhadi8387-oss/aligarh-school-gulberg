Practical Rules & Aptitude Test
  • Only one subject with practical is allowed in F.A.
  • Practical subjects: Psychology, Fine Art, Health & Physical Education, Statistics, Geography, Library Science.
  • Students opting Geography & Statistics cannot take Fine Art.
  • English Literature & Fine Arts require passing a Matric-standard Aptitude Test before admission.
Change of Subject Rules
  • Subject changes are permitted strictly within the prescribed time limit announced by the college.
  • An official application on the prescribed form must be submitted to the Vice Principal only.
  • No subject change requests will be entertained after the deadline expires.
